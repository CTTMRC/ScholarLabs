## Q1 · Has any experimental spray-drying study embedded a mechanistic equation for atomization, droplet shrinkage, or particle formation in a data-fitted structural causal model, causal Bayesian network, or process graph, and then tested its predictions after deliberately changing a real dryer setting? Which settings and responses were tested on physical runs outside calibration? Include food and chemical applications as well as pharmaceutical spray drying.

Look for studies that fit or constrain a physical equation with process data and then predict the response to a new physical experiment. Include papers that do not use the terms causal inference or Bayesian network.
